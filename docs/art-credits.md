# Art and palette credits

The potion, woodland creature, sparkle, meadow tile, and Meadow/Ember/Tide/Orchard palettes in `dist/studio-art.js` and `dist/studio-model.js` were created for Sprite Forge. They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . They may be edited, exported, and used in personal or commercial projects without attribution.

The Bellworn, Gutter Imp, Cinder Widow, Maw, Hollow Bride, and Briar Oracle procedural character designs were created for Sprite Forge and are also dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. These designs appeared in a retired local generator preview; any saved artwork remains usable. The original 48px renderer is retained for importing legacy projects without changing their artwork. No external sprite library, generated-image service, fonts, or remote image assets are used.
