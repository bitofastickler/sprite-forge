# Art and palette credits

The potion, woodland creature, sparkle, meadow tile, and Meadow/Ember/Tide/Orchard palettes in `dist/studio-art.js` and `dist/studio-model.js` were created for Sprite Forge. They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . They may be edited, exported, and used in personal or commercial projects without attribution.

The existing procedural character artwork is retained from Sprite Forge's original generator. The new part layers reproduce its original rendering pixel for pixel. No external sprite library, generated-image service, fonts, or remote image assets are used.
